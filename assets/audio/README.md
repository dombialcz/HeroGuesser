Seven placement effects extracted from the user-supplied `Single Thud.wav`.
Each main impact is a separate mono 48 kHz / 16-bit WAV, with peak normalization,
a 1 ms attack fade and a 20 ms tail fade. The fifth retains its natural double impact.
Silence between takes and the later handling noise after the first hit are excluded.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/split-placement-thuds.py /path/to/Single\ Thud.wav`.
The source recording is not modified or required at runtime.
