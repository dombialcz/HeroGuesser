# Naming research — 25 October 2026

**Publisher selection: Guild of Wits.** Pronunciation: “gild ov wits”; approximate Polish guide: „gild ow łits”. Short, pronounceable for both audiences, with fantasy guild identity and a reasoning theme. Keep the title in English in both locales. It avoids making the game sound like a trivia-only hero guessing game.

Exact-name web searches, including App Store/Google Play domain searches for Guild of Wits, did not surface an obvious exact game listing during this session. This is preliminary discovery evidence, not a reservation, trademark opinion or guarantee. Confirm availability in App Store Connect and review relevant brand rights before submission.
